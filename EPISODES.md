# Episode guide

36 scenes, 60 minutes, looping. Layout key: **4-pane** = one picture across all four panes, **Pair** = each window's upper+lower sash is one tall scene (left and right windows run separately), **Solo** = each pane plays its own mini-scene. The meeting rail and wall strip get no light, so characters vanish behind them and reappear on the other side.

Most scenes repeat a short gag (12–25 s) several times, so anyone passing sees a complete joke.

## Act I: Trick or Treat (about 12 min, cute and funny)

| # | Scene | Layout | Length | What you see |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bat Swarm | 4-pane | 80s | Purple bats flutter across a starry sky and a glowing moon. Every 20 s a flock bursts out of the moon. |
| 2 | Sneezing Pumpkins | Solo | 90s | A jack-o-lantern sits on each pane's sill. One inhales, sneezes "ACHOO!" and sprays seeds. The next one flinches and sneezes, and so on around the window. |
| 3 | Tangled Ghost | Pair | 90s | Left: a ghost floats down, gets stuck on the rail, struggles, then boings free and bounces. Right: a small ghost behind a "BOO" tombstone laughs, then flees. |
| 4 | Spider Fail | Pair | 80s | A spider lowers on a thread to scare a pumpkin (left) or skull (right). It yawns. The spider droops and is yanked back up. |
| 5 | Falling Apart | 4-pane | 100s | A big skeleton dances, then its skull flies across the window. It stumbles after it, puts it back upside down, and spins it round. It repeats from the other side. |
| 6 | Grim Calendar | Pair | 90s | Left: a hooded Reaper with a scythe. Right: an October calendar rolls down to the real number of days left, with an hourglass below. |
| 7 | Cat Knocks Pumpkin | Pair | 90s | A black cat on a shelf stares at you and pushes a pumpkin off the edge. It falls past the rail and bounces on the sill, and the cat doesn't care. A new pumpkin sparkles in. |
| 8 | Candy Corn Conga | 4-pane | 80s | Smiling candy corn march across the lower panes, and a second line marches the other way on top. |

| # | Scene | Layout | Length | What you see |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Haunted Mansion | Solo | 120s | Each pane is a lit mansion window with black silhouettes: a top-hatted walker, a candle-bearer, waltzing dancers, an organist, a waving ghost, glowing red eyes, a giant spider, flying bats. The lights go out between scenes. |
| 10 | Graveyard Rave | 4-pane | 110s | Sweeping neon beams, two disco balls, bobbing pumpkins and bats above, four skeletons dancing among tombstones. |
| 11 | Zombie Hands | Solo | 90s | Green hands rise from the dirt in each pane. One gropes, one gives a thumbs-up, one waves, and one pane has two hands doing jazz hands. |
| 12 | Witch Crash | 4-pane | 100s | A witch on a broom flies into the wall strip and slides down it. She sits dizzy with stars, stands up, and flies off. The next pass comes from the other side. |
| 13 | Bubbling Cauldron | Pair | 90s | Left: a green cauldron releases a little ghost that floats up the window. Right: a purple cauldron lets up a pink eyeball. Steam and bubbles in both. |
| 14 | Lightning Lab | 4-pane | 100s | Rain and lightning bolts that light up a Frankenstein's monster in the lower-left pane. Sometimes "IT'S ALIVE!" appears. |
| 15 | Vampire No Reflection | Pair | 100s | Left: a vampire shows off his fangs, fixes his hair, flexes and shrugs. Right: a gold mirror shows only his empty cape. |
| 16 | Werewolf Moon | Pair | 100s | Right: the moon swells while a man on a hill turns into a wolf and howls. Left: a moonlit forest with eyes opening in the trees. |
| 17 | Mummy Unwrap | 4-pane | 110s | A mummy spins while his bandage unspools across both windows, until a skeleton in sunglasses is left. Then he is re-wrapped. |

## Act III: Something's Watching (about 16 min, creepy)

| # | Scene | Layout | Length | What you see |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 | Eyes in the Dark | 4-pane | 100s | Pairs of glowing eyes open one at a time across the panes. They blink and slowly turn to look at the viewer. Late on, a huge red pair appears. |
| 19 | Fogged Glass | Pair | 100s | Fog on the glass. Left: child-sized handprints press on and streak down. Right: "HELP" is written in the fog and drips, and a hand drags down the lower pane. |
| 20 | Cracks | Solo | 100s | Cracks spread across each pane over a red glow, and a giant eye looks through one of them. Then the cracks heal. |
| 21 | The Shadow Approaches | Pair | 100s | Moonlit fog. A thin figure with white eyes walks toward you for 90 seconds, then a black hand presses on the glass. Right: a second figure that steps closer each time you look back. |
| 22 | Spider Infestation | 4-pane | 100s | Dozens of spiders crawl across all the panes, webs fill the corners, and a giant spider crosses mid-scene. |
| 23 | The Watchers | Solo | 90s | UL: a moon with a face that turns its eyes toward you. UR: an owl turning its head. LL: a swinging lantern with moths. LR: a pumpkin patch where the middle pumpkin slowly turns to face you. |
| 24 | Crows on the Rail | 4-pane | 100s | Crows land one by one on the rail and the fence. They go quiet, all look the same way, then all take off except one, which stays behind with red eyes. |
| 25 | Creeping Tendrils | 4-pane | 100s | Black tentacles with an eye on each tip creep up through a toxic green glow, across the rail, and back. |
| 26 | Giant Eye | Solo | 90s | A bloodshot eyeball slides into one pane at a time, looks around, and blinks sideways. The other panes glow dull red. |
| 27 | Flickering Hall | Solo | 90s | A silhouette in each pane (tall man, child with red balloon, crawler, long-haired woman). Each time the light dips, it is closer. Then the lights go out and it is gone. |

## Act IV: Finale (about 17 min, big set pieces, then a soft landing)

| # | Scene | Layout | Length | What you see |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 28 | Skeleton Army | 4-pane | 120s | A line of skeletons marches under a string of pumpkins. They trip over each other like dominoes into a bony pile, say "oof", then get up and march on. |
| 29 | Monster Stomp | 4-pane | 120s | Two giant legs stomp across a sunset skyline with screen shake and dust. Then its face lowers into the upper panes with red eyes, and it roars with a toothy mouth. |
| 30 | Ghost Parade & DJ | 4-pane | 100s | Neon ghosts conga across the panes with a visualizer and confetti. A ghost DJ in headphones plays on spinning turntables. |
| 31 | The Final Countdown | Pair | 100s | Left: a gothic clock showing laptop time, a skull pendulum, circling bats. Right: a fiery "N DAYS" and hh:mm:ss to Halloween. On Oct 31 it says "TONIGHT" with a pumpkin and fireworks. In November it says "SEE YOU NEXT YEAR". |
| 32 | Pumpkin Inflation | 4-pane | 120s | A worried pumpkin inflates until it fills every pane, shakes, and pops into a rain of candy. |
| 33 | Music Box | Solo | 90s | LL: a skeleton ballerina spins in a music box. UL: musical notes drift up. UR and LR: candles. |
| 34 | BOO! | Solo | 100s | Quiet fireflies. Then, every 25 s, a huge ghost bursts into one pane shouting "BOO!". Then four tiny ghosts peek up giggling. |
| 35 | Fireworks | 4-pane | 110s | Fireworks in round, ring, pumpkin and bat shapes. |
| 36 | Sweet Dreams | 4-pane | 140s | A single candle burns. "Sweet dreams..." writes itself in one pane, a red "?" appears, and a pair of eyes opens in another. The candle is blown out and everything goes dark. The loop then restarts with the bat swarm. |
